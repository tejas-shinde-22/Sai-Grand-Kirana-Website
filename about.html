Our story

A shop the community grew up with.

Sai Grand Kirana and General Store began with a simple idea — that a neighbourhood deserves a store it can truly rely on. Over the years, we’ve become part of daily life in Nimgaon Tembhi: the first stop in the morning and the last one at night.

We stock what real households need, keep our produce fresh, price things fairly, and greet every customer like a neighbour — because they are.

What we stand for

Four promises we never break.

01

Freshness we stand behind

Fruits, vegetables and daily staples are checked for quality every day. If we wouldn’t serve it at our own table, it doesn’t reach your basket.

02

Fair prices, every day

Honest, consistent pricing on the things you buy most — no surprises, no games. Good value is why families keep coming back.

03

Everything under one roof

From groceries to household and personal-care essentials, our shelves are organised so you find what you need — and get home faster.

04

A friendly face you know

We remember our regulars, help you find things, and are always a quick WhatsApp message away for anything you need.
